## The idea

Explore the rhythm of public transport across metropolitan Adelaide, from quieter periods to the busiest parts of the day.

This is a planned experiment. The transport animation is not available yet.

## Data and method

Public timetable and vehicle-position data are candidates for this project. Access terms and coverage still need to be checked, and scheduled movement will need to be clearly distinguished from observed movement. No dataset has been analysed and there are no findings to report yet.
