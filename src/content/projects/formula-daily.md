## Prototype notes

This early interaction prototype tests the playing surface: select the empty spreadsheet cell, then place mixed function, range, value, and punctuation pieces directly into it by click, tap, keyboard, or optional dragging.

Formula checking is intentionally not active yet. During this review stage, the submit action records an attempt so the construction flow can be tested without treating the sample as a finished puzzle.

## Direction

The eventual game will contain five increasingly difficult questions and evaluate a player's permitted spreadsheet expression against the puzzle inputs. It will emulate a deliberately small spreadsheet grammar rather than store one inspectable expected formula or attempt to reproduce all of Excel.
