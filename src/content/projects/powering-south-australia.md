## The idea

Explore how generation, demand and energy flows change through the day, and how different sources contribute to South Australia's electricity supply.

This is a planned experiment. The energy timeline is not available yet.

## Data and method

A suitable public electricity dataset still needs to be selected and its usage terms confirmed. Time intervals, generation categories and treatment of interstate flows will be documented before analysis. No dataset has been analysed and there are no findings to report yet.
