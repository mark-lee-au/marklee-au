# Project Backlog

This is an idea bank, not a commitment list.

## Fuel and mobility

### The Pulse of Adelaide

Animate fuel price changes across Adelaide through a full price cycle.

Possible encodings:

- station position = geography
- pulse = price change event
- intensity = relative price
- timeline = observed time
- symbol or detail = brand

Likely category after completion: MAPS

### Adelaide in Motion

Animate buses, trains, and trams across Adelaide using GTFS and real-time feeds.

Likely category: MAPS

### Commute City

Show how travel time to central Adelaide changes by mode, time, or suburb.

Likely category: MAPS

## Energy

### Powering South Australia

Animate generation mix, demand, price, storage, and interconnector flows over a day.

Likely category: DATA or MAPS depending on final representation.

### When Solar Takes Over

Show the daily periods when rooftop and utility solar dominate South Australian electricity supply.

Likely category: DATA
