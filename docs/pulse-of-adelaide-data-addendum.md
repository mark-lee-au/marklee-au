# Pulse of Adelaide: historical data contract

Plan 002 is authoritative. The active local-only view is an event-based historical visualisation backed by the original R Parquet and the corrected South Australian site lookup. The old 40-station archive and daily browser importer are superseded for active playback.

The corrected lookup contains 1,219 SA sites, 757 matched to the national historical IDs, 311 matched in the Adelaide region. February 2024 ULP has 295 reporting stations. Source records are kept unchanged in the user's private data; the R exporter groups exact duplicate station/time records while retaining their multiplicity, preserves repeated same-price reports and all price changes, and writes per-month/per-grade files with a 48-hour seed lookback. The browser reconstructs the latest known price per station at each moment and shows an unweighted mean across known stations. Unobserved is not zero, and time between reports is explicitly carried forward rather than independently observed.

The source SQL appends `Z` after formatting `TRANSACTIONDATE`, without proving it represents UTC. Do not silently shift timestamps or assert precise effective price-change times. Generated browser assets are ignored by Git but enter Astro builds if left under `public/`; local review only until supplier identity and reuse rights are verified. The SA Government Direct API and its publisher agreement remain a separate project, Plan 008.
